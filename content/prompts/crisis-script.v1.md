## title

Thank you for telling me

## body

What you've said matters, and it sounds really hard. You deserve proper support from a real person, and I'm an AI, so I'm not the right one to help with this.

Please talk to an adult you trust as soon as you can. That could be a parent or carer, a teacher, or someone else in your family. You don't have to find the perfect words. You can just show them this message.

You can also talk to someone right now. These are free, and the people there are kind and used to listening:

## contacts

- name: Childline
  action: call
  display: 0800 1111
  href: tel:08001111
  detail: Free, any time, for anyone under 19. It won't show on a phone bill.
- name: Samaritans
  action: call
  display: 116 123
  href: tel:116123
  detail: Free, any time, day or night.
- name: Shout
  action: text
  display: Text SHOUT to 85258
  href: sms:85258?body=SHOUT
  detail: Free text support, any time.

## emergency

If you or someone else is in danger right now, call 999.

- name: Emergency services
  action: call
  display: 999
  href: tel:999
  detail: For immediate danger.

## closing

You've done the right thing by saying something. Someone on our safety team will also be told, so that a person can check you're okay. You're not in trouble.
