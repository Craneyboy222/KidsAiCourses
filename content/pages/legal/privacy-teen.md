This page is for you. It says what we know about you and what we do with it. Your parent or carer has a longer version: the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy).

# What we know about you

- Your nickname and your username. Not your real name.
- The month and year you were born. Not the day.
- The courses you do, your answers, your notes and the work you hand in.
- What you type to Coach, and what Coach says back.
- How long you spend here.
- How you like the site to look, such as text size.

# What we never ask you for

- Your email address
- Your full name, school or home address
- A photo of you
- A password or code for any other app or website

If anything here asks you for one of these, tell us with the Report button.

# What we do with it

- We save your place so you can carry on later.
- We keep your finished projects in your portfolio.
- We make your certificates.
- We check messages and work to keep you and others safe.

We do not show you adverts. We do not sell what we know about you.

# Who can see it

- **You.**
- **Your parent or carer.** They see your progress, your time, your projects and certificates, and how many Coach chats you have had and what they were about. They do not see what you typed to Coach, your notes or your password.
- **Our team**, only when they need to. For example, to check work you hand in or to answer a report. We keep a record each time.
- **Other learners: never.** Nobody else here can see you or message you.

Some companies help us run the site, for example to store data or to power Coach. They are only allowed to use your data to do that job for us. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: the list of companies.

# Coach

Coach is an AI. Before your message goes to it, we take out personal details we can spot. What you said is deleted after 30 days. Your parent can choose to keep it for 90 days so you can look back further.

# If we are worried about you

If something you write suggests you or someone else might be in danger, we show you where to get help. A trained person on our team, called the safeguarding lead, is told. They might contact your parent or carer, or someone else who can help keep you safe. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: wording agreed with the safeguarding lead.

# Tools from other companies

When you make something in another company's tool, what you type there goes to that company, not to us. Each tool has its own privacy rules. We only get what you choose to hand in.

# What you can do

- See what your parent can see: go to Settings.
- Ask your parent or carer to download your data or delete your account. They can do both from their dashboard.
- Ask us a question, or tell us something is wrong, with the Report button.
- When you are 18, you can do all of this yourself.

You have rights over your data, including to see it and to have mistakes fixed. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: how a young person can use these rights without a parent.

# How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is open. If your parent asks us to delete it, it is closed straight away and removed after 14 days. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: all time periods.

# Cookies

We only use the cookies the site needs to work. There are no advertising cookies. See the [cookie notice](/legal/cookies).
