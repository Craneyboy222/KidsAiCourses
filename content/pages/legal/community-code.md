{{brandName}} is a place to make things. These are the rules that keep it a good one. They apply to everything you type, upload or hand in.

# 1. Keep yourself and others safe

- Do not share personal details: your full name, address, phone number, school or social media accounts.
- Do not hand in photos or videos that show real people, including you. Use the sample images, cartoons, objects or generated pictures that do not show a real person.
- Never share a password, sign-in code or key, for this site or any other.
- If someone is in danger, press Report and choose "Someone is in danger", or call 999.

# 2. Be honest about your work

- Hand in work you made. Using AI tools is the point of the courses, so say where you used them: add a "Made with AI" note where the course asks for one.
- Do not pass off AI-made work as your own for school, coursework or exams.
- Do not copy someone else's work and call it yours.

# 3. Respect other people

- No bullying, threats, hate or harassment, in anything you make or write.
- Do not make content that pretends to be a real person, or that shows a real person saying or doing something they did not.
- Do not make content that is sexual, violent or cruel, or that encourages self-harm, illegal acts or dangerous challenges.

# 4. Respect other people's work

- Only use images, music, video and text that you made, that the course gave you, or that you have permission to use.
- Do not upload copyrighted music or films.

# 5. Use the tools properly

- Follow the age rules and terms of each outside tool. If a tool needs a parent's OK, ask for it.
- Do not try to get round a parent's limits, the checks on Coach, or someone else's account.
- Do not use Coach to get homework, coursework or exam answers written for you.

# 6. What happens if the code is broken

- Work that breaks the code is not accepted, and we may remove it.
- We may tell your parent or carer.
- For serious or repeated problems we may pause or close an account.

⚖️ TO CONFIRM: how decisions are explained, how to ask for one to be looked at again, and what is reported to outside authorities.

# 7. If you see something that breaks the code

Press Report. A person on our team will look at it. You will not get in trouble for reporting something honestly.
