# The short version

We only use cookies that the site needs in order to work. There are **no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no tracking by other companies** on our pages. Because of that, we do not show a cookie banner asking for consent. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: that every item below is "strictly necessary", so that no consent is required.

# Cookies we set

- **Sign-in session** (`better-auth.session_token`). Keeps you signed in as you move between pages. Set when you sign in; removed when you sign out. It lasts up to 7 days from your last visit and never more than 30 days in total, or 12 hours for staff. It cannot be read by scripts.
- **Accessibility settings** (`a11y`). Remembers the text size, colours, reading mode and motion setting you chose, so pages look right from the first moment. It holds those four settings and nothing that identifies you. It is set only when you change a setting or sign in to an account that has saved settings, and lasts one year. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: treatment as a strictly necessary cookie.
- **Age check** (`age_gate`). If a birth month and year show someone is under 13, this stops the sign-up form being tried again straight away. It holds no personal data and lasts one hour.
- **Two-step sign-in** (staff only). Short-lived cookies used while a member of staff enters their verification code.

# Other storage in your browser

- **Low-data video setting.** If you turn on low-data mode for videos, your browser remembers that choice on that device.
- **Offline page.** If your browser supports it, we store a small copy of our "You're offline" page and the site's styles on your device, so you see a helpful message if your connection drops. It contains nothing about you or your account, and it is cleared when you sign out.

# Cookies from other companies

Our own pages do not load content, fonts, scripts or trackers from other companies.

If a parent or adult learner goes to pay, that happens on a page run by Stripe, which sets its own cookies under its own policy. Learners under 18 are never sent there.

Tools from other companies that are taught in our courses open on those companies' own websites, with their own cookies.

# Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block our session cookie, you will not be able to sign in.

# Changes

If we ever want to use a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will ask first and update this notice. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM.
