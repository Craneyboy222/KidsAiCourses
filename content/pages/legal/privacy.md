# 2. The short version

- We collect as little as we can about young people: no email address, full name, school, address or photograph.
- We do not show adverts, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising.
- Learners cannot see or contact each other.
- A parent or carer can download or delete their child's data at any time.

# 3. What we collect

**About a parent or carer**

- name and email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash)
- your consent choices, with the date and the wording you agreed to
- your plan and payment status. Card details are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach us.
- messages and requests you send us

**About a learner**

- nickname and username (not a real name), and a password (stored only as a one-way hash)
- birth month and year, never the full date, and the age band worked out from it
- interests chosen at set-up, a generated avatar and accessibility settings
- courses started and finished, progress, quiz answers, notes, reflections and time spent
- work handed in: links, screenshots, files and videos, and the outcome of checks on them
- messages to Coach, our AI helper, and its replies (see section 7)
- reports they make, and any safeguarding record (see section 8)
- requests they send a parent, such as to approve a tool

**About everyone who uses the site**

- technical data needed to run and protect the service: IP address, browser type, and security and error logs
- strictly necessary cookies (see the [cookie notice](/legal/cookies))

**About under-13s:** if a birth month and year show a child is under 13, sign-up stops and we store nothing about the child, only an anonymous count.

# 4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

⚖️ TO CONFIRM: every lawful basis in this section, and the outcome of the data protection impact assessment (DPIA).

- **To provide the account, courses, portfolio and certificates.** Proposed basis: contract with the parent or adult learner, and consent given by the parent for the child's account.
- **To run Coach.** Proposed basis: consent given by the parent in the consent centre.
- **To keep young people safe**: moderation of Coach messages and submitted work, reports and safeguarding. Proposed basis: legitimate interests and legal obligation; for any special category data, ⚖️ TO CONFIRM the condition relied on (safeguarding of children and individuals at risk).
- **To check an adult is involved** (age assurance). Proposed basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: the age assurance method.
- **To take payment and keep billing records** (parents and adult learners only). Proposed basis: contract and legal obligation.
- **To secure the service and prevent misuse.** Proposed basis: legitimate interests.
- **To improve courses using pseudonymised usage records**, only where a parent has switched on the optional consent. Basis: consent.

We do not use young people's data for marketing, and we do not send marketing email to under-18s.

# 5. Who we share it with

We use other companies ("processors") to run the service. Each acts only on our instructions under a written agreement. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: the final list, with each company's name, purpose and location, once providers are approved:

- website and application hosting
- database hosting
- file storage for submitted work
- email delivery (to parents and staff only)
- payment processing: Stripe (parents and adult learners only)
- AI model access for Coach: an AI gateway and a model provider
- error monitoring

We may also share information where the law requires it, or with the police, children's social care or another safeguarding body where we believe a child is at risk of harm. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: reporting duties and process.

We do not share personal data with the companies whose tools are taught in our courses. If a learner creates an account with one of those companies, that company is the controller of what they give it.

# 6. Where data is stored

Our intention is to store account data in the United Kingdom. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: hosting region for each processor. Messages to Coach are processed by an AI provider; ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: where that processing takes place and the safeguards for any transfer outside the UK.

# 7. Coach, our AI helper

A learner's message goes to our servers first. We check it, remove personal details we can detect, and refuse anything that looks like a password or sign-in code. It is then sent, with the course step, to the AI provider, and the reply is checked before it is shown. Where a provider offers settings that stop it keeping messages or using them to train models, we switch them on. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: the provider's retention and training terms.

Parents cannot read Coach conversations. They see a count, topics and any safety notice.

# 8. Safety checks, reports and safeguarding

Messages to Coach and submitted work are checked automatically. These checks can flag or hold content; a person on our team decides anything that affects an account. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: whether any of this is automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effect.

If a message or report suggests a young person may be at risk, a safeguarding record is created and our Designated Safeguarding Lead is alerted. Safeguarding notes are encrypted and access to them is logged.

# 9. How long we keep it

⚖️ TO CONFIRM: every period below. These are the product's current defaults.

- Account, progress and portfolio: for as long as the account is open; deleted 14 days after a deletion request; or after 24 months without activity, with a warning to the parent 30 days before.
- Coach conversations: the words for 30 days after the session (90 days if the parent has switched on longer history), then only counts and topics.
- Technical records of AI use: 13 months, then only totals.
- Content flagged by moderation: 12 months after it is resolved.
- Safeguarding records: under our safeguarding policy. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM.
- Billing records: 6 years (parents and adult learners only).
- Audit logs: 2 years.
- A teenager's sign-up that a parent has not approved: 7 days.
- Consent records: ⚖️ TO CONFIRM how long evidence of consent is kept after an account is deleted.

# 10. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing a consent the account needs pauses the account.

Parents can download or delete a child's data from the Privacy page of the parent dashboard. A parent's download leaves out the child's private writing (Coach messages, notes and reflections). ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: how a parent's request for that material on a child's behalf is handled, and how a young person exercises their own rights.

To use any right, contact us at the address in section 1. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

# 11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, safeguarding notes are encrypted before they are stored, staff sign in with two-step verification, and access to learner records by staff is logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: encryption at rest for each hosting provider, and the breach notification process.

# 12. Changes

We will update this notice when what we do changes, and tell parents about significant changes before they take effect. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: how notice is given.
