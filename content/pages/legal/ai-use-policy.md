We teach young people to use AI well, so we should be clear about how we use it ourselves.

# Where we use AI

1. **Coach**, the helper inside courses.
2. **Drafting courses.** AI helps us write first drafts of courses and setup guides.
3. **Finding tools.** An automated fetcher looks for new tools and for changes to tools we already teach. See [Tool Radar](/radar).
4. **Safety checks.** Automated checks on Coach messages and on work that is handed in.

# What people do

- **A person signs off every course.** No AI-drafted content is published until someone on our team has reviewed it, tested it as a learner would and approved it. Published courses show "Reviewed and signed off by the team" and the date they were last checked.
- **A person decides what happens to an account.** Automated checks can hold or flag content. They do not close accounts.
- **A person reads reports.**

# Coach: the rules it follows

- It always says it is an AI. Every reply is labelled as AI-generated.
- It gives hints in steps: what have you tried, a nudge, a clue, a similar example, the idea behind it. It does not give the full solution or the finished project.
- It refuses to write homework, coursework, essays or exam answers. It offers to explain, plan, quiz or give feedback instead.
- It does not ask for personal information, and never for a password, sign-in code or key.
- It does not give medical, legal or financial advice, and does not do romantic role-play.
- It does not discourage talking to a trusted adult.
- If a message suggests someone may be at risk of harm, it shows a fixed message with where to get help, and our safeguarding lead is alerted.

# What happens to messages

Messages go through our servers. We remove personal details we can detect before a message is sent to the AI provider, and we check replies before they are shown. We only use AI providers approved by us with a data-processing agreement, and we switch on settings that stop a provider keeping messages or training on them wherever those are offered. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: the provider, where processing takes place, and its retention and training terms.

Learners' messages and work are not used by us to train AI models. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM.

# What AI gets wrong

AI can state things that are false, miss context and reflect unfair bias. Our checks can miss things too. We ask learners to check anything important, and we give every AI reply a Report button.

# What we expect from learners

- Be open about what AI did and what you did. Add a "Made with AI" note where a course asks for one.
- Do not use AI to create a likeness of a real person.
- Do not present AI-made work as your own for school or exams.
- Follow the [community code](/legal/community-code).

# Tools from other companies

Many tools in our courses have their own AI features. Those are run by the other company under its own rules, not by us, and this policy does not cover them. We record each tool's published rules before a course goes live.

We will update this policy when how we use AI changes. ⚖️ TO CONFIRM: review schedule and owner.
