This page describes what the product does today. It is not a promise that nothing can ever go wrong: no online service can honestly say that. Where there are limits, we say so.

# Accounts and age

- **Ages 13 to 18 only.** Anyone under 13 is stopped at sign-up. We keep nothing about them, only an anonymous count of how many times it happened.
- **A parent or carer is always attached to an under-18 account.** A parent creates the family account and adds each child, or approves a sign-up the teenager started. A sign-up that is not approved within 7 days is deleted.
- **We ask for very little.** For a learner: a nickname, a username and a birth month and year. No email address, full name, school, address or photograph. Usernames are checked so they do not give away personal details.
- **Checking an adult is involved.** For 13 to 15-year-olds, a parent completes a card check (no money is taken) or holds a paid plan before the child can use Coach. For 16 and 17-year-olds, a parent's verified email address and consent are needed.
- **Learners sign in with a username and password.** If a password is forgotten, a parent resets it from their dashboard. After several wrong attempts, sign-in is paused for a short time.

# Private by default

- No public profiles, no friends lists, no direct messages, no comments and no leaderboards. Learners cannot see each other at all.
- Avatars are generated shapes, never photographs.
- Portfolios are private: only the learner and their linked parents can open them.
- Learner pages are not listed by search engines.
- No adverts, and we do not sell or share young people's data for marketing. We do not send marketing email to under-18s.

# Consent

Before a child's account is used, a parent goes through the consent centre in plain English. Three consents are needed for an account to work: keeping account and learning data, Coach's AI processing, and the use of tools from other companies. Two are optional and are off unless the parent switches them on: keeping Coach conversations for longer, and product-improvement analytics. A parent can change these at any time. Withdrawing a consent the account needs pauses the account straight away, and the child's work is kept until the parent decides what to do.

# Tools from other companies

Learners make their projects in real tools run by other companies. This is the part of the service we control least, so here is exactly what we do and do not do.

**What we do**

- Every course lists each tool it uses, with the official link, the cost or free plan, the minimum age in that company's own terms, and whether a parent must approve it or run the account.
- A person on our team checks and records those facts before a course can be published. A tool whose terms do not allow under-18s is either run by the parent or not used at all.
- Where a tool needs a parent's permission, the course cannot be started until the parent approves that tool for that course. Approval can be withdrawn later.
- Links to tools go only to the official addresses we have recorded, and learners are told when they are leaving {{brandName}}.
- We never ask for a password, sign-in code or key for another company's tool. Coach refuses if someone tries to type one in.

**What we cannot do**

- We do not run those tools, and we cannot see what a learner types or makes inside them. That goes to the other company under its own terms and privacy policy.
- We cannot promise that another company's tool is safe, will stay free, or will stay the same. Tools change. Each guide shows the date it was last checked, and learners can report a guide that no longer matches.

# Coach, the AI helper

- Coach always says it is an AI. Every reply is labelled as AI-generated.
- It gives hints, asks questions and explains. It does not write the project, and it refuses to write homework, coursework, essays or exam answers.
- Messages go from the browser to our servers first. We check each message, remove personal details we can detect, and refuse anything that looks like a password or sign-in code. Only then is it sent to the AI company that works for us. The reply is checked again before the learner sees it. Checks are stricter for 13 to 15-year-olds.
- If a message suggests a young person may be at risk of harm, they are shown a fixed message, written and approved in advance rather than generated, with where to get help straight away. Our safeguarding lead is alerted.
- Parents do not read Coach conversations. They see how many there were, the topics, and any safety notice.
- By default the content of a conversation is deleted 30 days after the session; only a count and topics are kept. A conversation that raised a safety concern is kept for longer and can be read only by our safeguarding lead.
- Coach use has a monthly allowance for each learner, shown as "energy". There is also an overall daily limit; if it is reached, Coach takes a break and the rest of the course carries on working.

**The limit:** automated checks are not perfect. They can miss something worrying, and they can react to something harmless. That is why every Coach reply has a Report button and why a person, not software, decides what happens next.

# Checks on work that is handed in

- Every submission goes through automated checks first: file type and size, link checks and content checks.
- Photos and videos that show real people are not accepted, and courses are not allowed to ask for them. Image and video files are looked at by a person before they are accepted.
- Clean links and documents can be accepted without waiting; a share of them is also checked by a person. Anything flagged waits for a person.
- If work is sent back, the files in that submission are deleted from our storage and the learner can try again.
- We never open, host or run a learner's app. A link is stored as a link.

# Reporting a concern

- There is a Report control on every step, every course, every project and every Coach reply.
- "Someone is in danger" goes straight to our safeguarding lead and shows where to get help immediately.
- A learner can see the reports they have made, and what happened to them, in Settings.
- We are a small team. Reports are read by a person, but not instantly, and not through the night.

**If you or someone else is in danger right now, call 999.** You can also talk to Childline free on 0800 1111, call Samaritans on 116 123, or text SHOUT to 85258, at any time.

# What parents control

A parent can set a daily time limit and quiet hours, block categories or single courses, ask to approve harder courses first, approve tools one by one, pause the account (which signs the child out at once), reset the password, download the child's data and ask us to delete it. More in the [guide for parents](/parents).

# Cookies and tracking

We use only the cookies the site needs to work: to keep you signed in, to remember accessibility settings, and to remember that an under-13 was stopped at sign-up for a short time. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and no trackers or content loaded from other companies on our pages. See the [cookie notice](/legal/cookies).
