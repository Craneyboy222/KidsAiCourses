# Good to know

- **The Free plan stays free.** It is not a trial. Free courses are marked "Free" in the [catalogue](/courses?free=1), and they are complete courses with a certificate at the end.
- **Every plan includes every parental control.** Time limits, quiet hours, tool approvals, pausing, and data download and deletion are never an extra.
- **A parent or an adult pays, never a child.** Learners under 18 do not see prices, a checkout or invoices. If they want a course that is not on your plan, they can send you a request to unlock it.
- **Cancel when you like.** You manage or cancel a paid plan yourself from Billing in your dashboard. Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
- **Nothing is deleted when a plan ends.** Finished projects, portfolios and certificates are kept, and the free courses stay open.
- **Coach has a monthly allowance for each learner.** Paid plans include a bigger one. Unused allowance does not carry over to the next month.
- **Tools from other companies are separate.** Courses are designed to be finished on a tool's free plan. Where a tool or a feature costs money, the course says so before your child starts, and nothing you pay us goes towards another company's tool.
