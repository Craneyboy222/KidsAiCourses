# Straight answers

We would rather you knew what this is before you sign up than find out afterwards.

- **Videos are short at launch, and most steps are written.** Each course has a short trailer and a few short clips. The step-by-step instructions are text and pictures you can follow at your own speed. Every video has captions and a transcript.
- **Courses are drafted with AI and signed off by a person.** We use AI to help draft course content. A person on our team then reviews every course, tests it and signs it off before anyone can see it. Nothing is published without that sign-off, and each course shows the date it was last checked.
- **Coach gives hints. It does not do the work.** Coach is an AI helper built into the courses. It asks questions, explains ideas and nudges you towards the next step. It will not write your project, your homework or your coursework, and it always says it is an AI.
- **The tools belong to other companies.** You make your project in real tools such as app builders, design tools and video editors. Those companies set their own rules, ages and prices. We check and record each tool's rules before a course goes live, but we do not run those tools and we cannot promise what another company's product will do.
- **There is no feed, no chat and no public profile.** Learners cannot see or message each other. Finished work goes into a private portfolio that only the learner and their parent or carer can see.
- **It is for ages 13 to 18 only.** Anyone under 13 is stopped at sign-up, and we keep nothing about them.
