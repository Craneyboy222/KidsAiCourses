# The short version

{{brandName}} is an online studio for teenagers aged 13 to 18. Each course is one short project. You make it in a real tool, with our guide beside you, and you keep what you make.

# Step by step

1. **A parent or carer sets up the family.** They create the account with their email address, then add each teenager with a nickname, a username and a birth month and year. Teenagers do not need an email address. A teenager can also start a sign-up themselves, but the account stays locked until a parent approves it.
2. **Pick a project.** Browse the [courses](/courses) by what you want to make, how long you have and how hard you want it to be. Every course says what you will make, how long it takes and which tools it uses.
3. **Check "Tools you'll need".** Each course lists the outside tools it uses: the official link, what the tool costs or whether it has a free plan, the minimum age the company sets, and whether a parent has to approve it or run the account. If a tool needs a parent's OK, the course cannot be started until they have given it.
4. **Follow the steps.** A course is a mix of short videos, written steps, setup guides, tasks to do in the tool, quick quizzes and an ethics checkpoint. At launch the videos are short and most steps are written. The work itself happens in the tool, in another tab; your place here is saved automatically, so you can stop and come back on any device.
5. **Ask Coach when you are stuck.** Coach is our built-in AI helper. Press "I'm stuck" and it starts with the step's own hints, then asks what you have tried and nudges you forward. It does not do the project for you. See [how Coach works](/help/how-coach-works).
6. **Think it through.** Every course has an ethics checkpoint: a short stop to think about who made what, what is fair to use, and how to be honest about using AI.
7. **Hand in your work.** At the end you send us a link, a screenshot or a file, depending on the course. It is checked before it is accepted. Please do not send photos or videos that show real people, including you: we do not accept them.
8. **Keep it.** Accepted work appears in your private portfolio, and you get a certificate with a link anyone can use to check it is real. The check page shows a first name or nickname, the course and the date, and nothing else.

# How the courses are made

- We use AI to help draft courses, and to look for new tools worth teaching. An AI draft is only ever a draft.
- A person on our team reviews every course against a checklist, tests it from start to finish as a learner would, and signs it off. **Nothing is published without that sign-off.**
- Every course shows "Reviewed and signed off by the team" and the date it was last checked. Tools change often; when a guide no longer matches what you see, use the Report button on the step and we will fix it.

# What it is not

- It is not a social network. There are no public profiles, no direct messages, no feed and no leaderboards.
- It is not a homework machine. Coach will explain, quiz you and give feedback, but it will not write schoolwork.
- It is not a coding sandbox or an app host. You build in real tools, with your own account on those tools where their rules allow it. We never hold your sign-in details for them.
- It is not for under-13s.

# What you need

- A phone, tablet, laptop or Chromebook with a web browser. You can add {{brandName}} to your home screen like an app.
- A parent or carer to set up or approve the account.
- For each course, the tools it lists. Courses are designed to be finished on a tool's free plan; where something costs money, the course says so clearly before you start.
