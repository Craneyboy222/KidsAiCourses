# What your child actually does

Your child picks a project they care about, such as an app, a short film or a chatbot, and follows a guided course to make it in a real tool. A course is short, self-paced and ends with something finished that they keep in a private portfolio, with a certificate.

Two things to know up front:

- **At launch, course videos are short and most steps are written.** The teaching is mainly clear written steps with pictures, plus short clips with captions.
- **AI helps us draft courses, and a person signs off every one.** Nothing is published until someone on our team has reviewed and tested it. Each course shows when it was last checked.

# You stay in charge

From your dashboard you can, for each child:

- **Set a daily time limit**, from 5 minutes to 12 hours. When time is up, their work is saved and the session stops.
- **Set quiet hours**, when learning is switched off and no notifications are sent to them.
- **Block categories or single courses.** A blocked course simply does not appear for them.
- **Ask to approve harder ("Pro") courses** before they start.
- **Approve tools one at a time.** If a course uses a tool that needs your permission, your child cannot start it until you say yes. You can withdraw approval later.
- **Pause the account.** This signs them out everywhere straight away.
- **Reset their password**, or give them a one-time code to set a new one.
- **Download their data or ask us to delete it.** Deletion can be undone for 14 days.

You can switch between children from any page of the dashboard.

# What you can see, and what stays private

You see enough to know your child is safe and making progress:

- courses started and finished, and how far through they are
- time spent this week
- projects in their portfolio and certificates earned
- how many Coach conversations they have had, and the topics
- safety notices, if something worrying came up
- anything they have asked you to approve

Some things stay private to them, so they can ask questions freely: what they typed to Coach and what it said back, their private notes, their reflections, and their password. Your child is shown the same list, so there are no surprises on either side. If something suggests your child may be in danger, our safeguarding lead may contact you. That is the only time more is shared.

# Tools from other companies, and your part in them

Courses teach real tools made by other companies. For every tool, the course shows the official link, what it costs or whether there is a free plan, the minimum age the company sets, and one of three things:

- **Your child can use it.** The company's terms allow their age.
- **It needs your OK.** You approve that tool for that course from your approvals inbox.
- **You run the account.** Some tools are for adults only. Where their terms allow it, you set up and operate the account and your child directs. The setup guide for these tools is written for you.

These tools have their own terms and privacy policies, which you or your child agree to with that company, not with us. What is typed into them goes to that company. We only receive what your child chooses to hand in: a link, a screenshot or a file. We will never ask for a password, sign-in code or key for one of these tools.

We check and record each tool's rules before a course goes live, but we cannot promise what another company's product will do or that it will stay the same. If a tool worries you, do not approve it; there are other courses.

# Coach, the built-in AI helper

Coach gives hints and explanations. It does not do the project, and it will not write homework or coursework. Messages are checked before they are sent to the AI company that works for us and again before your child sees a reply, and personal details we can detect are removed. If a message suggests your child may be at risk, they are shown where to get help straight away and our safeguarding lead is alerted. Automated checks are not perfect, which is why there is a Report button on every reply. More on the [Safety page](/safety).

# What we ask of you

1. **Create the family account** with your email address, and confirm it.
2. **Add each child**: a nickname, a username and their birth month and year. No email address is needed for them. Give them a password, or a one-time invite code that works for 72 hours.
3. **Go through the consent centre.** It explains in plain English what data we keep and why.
4. **For a child aged 13 to 15, complete a card check** before they use Coach. No money is taken; it shows an adult is involved. Starting a paid plan does the same job.

# What it costs

There is a free plan with a small set of complete courses and every parental control. A paid family plan opens the whole catalogue. Plans and current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing). Your child never sees prices or a checkout: if they want a course that is not on your plan, they can send you a request.

# If something goes wrong

- Use the Report control, on any step, course, project or Coach reply.
- Read the [help centre](/help), which has a section for parents.
- In an emergency, call 999. Childline is free on 0800 1111.
