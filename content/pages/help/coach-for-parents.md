Coach is the AI helper inside the courses. It is the only part of {{brandName}} where your child talks to an AI model inside our site.

# What it is for

It gives hints and explanations and asks questions. It is instructed not to complete the project and to refuse homework, coursework and exam answers. It always identifies itself as an AI, and every reply is labelled as AI-generated.

# Where messages go

1. Your child's message goes to our servers, never straight to an AI company.
2. We check it. Personal details we can detect are removed, and anything that looks like a password or sign-in code is refused.
3. It is sent, with the course step they are on, to an AI company that works for us under contract.
4. The reply is checked before your child sees it.

# If something worrying is said

A message that suggests a risk of harm shows your child a fixed message with where to get help straight away (Childline, Samaritans, Shout and 999). Our safeguarding lead is alerted, and may contact you.

# What you can see

How many conversations there were, the topics, and any safety notices. You cannot read the conversations. This is deliberate, so that teenagers can ask questions freely, and your child is told the same.

# How long conversations are kept

The words are deleted 30 days after the session, and we keep only a count and topics. You can choose in the consent centre to keep them for 90 days. A conversation that raised a safety concern is kept for longer and only our safeguarding lead can read it.

# The honest limits

Automated checks are not perfect and an AI can be wrong. Coach works alongside your own conversations with your child; it does not replace them. Every reply has a Report button.
