Some tools ask for a parent's permission before an under-18 uses them, and some are for adults only. Where that is the case, your child cannot start the course until you have decided.

# Your approvals inbox

Requests appear under **Approvals** in your dashboard, and we email you. Each request shows the course, the tool, its cost or free plan, the minimum age the company sets and a link to its official website.

# Two kinds of request

- **Approve the tool.** The company's terms allow your child to use it with a parent's permission. If you approve, they create their own account by following our guide.
- **You run the account.** The tool is for adults. Where its terms allow, you create and operate the account, and your child directs what to make. You confirm that you will set up and run it, and the setup guide is written for you: you can open it from the request.

# Deciding

- **Approve**: your child can start the course. Approval is for that tool on that course.
- **Decline**: the course stays unavailable. There are plenty of others.
- **Withdraw later**: you can take back an approval. Your child will not be able to continue the steps that use the tool.

# What approval does not mean

We check and record each tool's rules before a course is published, but the tool belongs to another company. Its terms and privacy policy are between you, your child and that company, and we cannot promise how its product behaves. Read the tool's own pages if you are unsure, and decline if you are not comfortable.
