---
title: What my parent can see
audience: teen
topic: consent
summary: Your parent or carer sees your progress and any safety notices. They do not see what you type to Coach or your private notes.
position: 100
---
Your parent or carer helps look after your account. They see enough to know you are safe and getting on, not everything you type.

# They can see

- the courses you have started and finished, and how far through you are
- how much time you have spent this week
- the projects in your portfolio and your certificates
- how many Coach chats you have had, and the topics
- safety notices, if something worrying came up
- anything you have asked them to approve, such as a tool or a locked course

# They cannot see

- what you typed to Coach, or what Coach said back
- your private notes on a step
- what you wrote in a reflection or an ethics checkpoint
- your password. They can reset it, but they can never read it.

# What they can change

They can set a daily time limit and quiet hours, block categories or courses, approve tools, pause your account, and download or delete your data.

# One exception

If something suggests you might be in danger, our safeguarding lead may contact your parent or carer. That is the only time more is shared.

You can read this list any time in **Settings**, under "What my parent can see". Your parent is shown the same list.
