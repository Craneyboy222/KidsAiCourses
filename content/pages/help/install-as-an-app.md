{{brandName}} is a website, but you can add it to your home screen or app list so it opens like an app. There is nothing to download from an app shop.

# How to add it

- **Chromebook, Windows or Mac (Chrome or Edge):** open the site, then choose the install icon in the address bar, or the browser menu and "Install".
- **Android (Chrome):** open the browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** press Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

# When you are offline

You need a connection to learn: courses, Coach and your work are not stored on the device. If the connection drops, you will see a short "You're offline" page. Your progress up to that point is already saved, so nothing is lost. Reconnect and carry on.

# On a shared device

Sign out when you finish. Nothing from your account is kept on the device after you sign out.
