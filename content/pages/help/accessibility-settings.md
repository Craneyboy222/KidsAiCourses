You can change how {{brandName}} looks so that it is comfortable to read.

# What you can change

- **Text size**: four sizes, up to one and a half times bigger.
- **Colours**: light, dark or high contrast.
- **Reading mode**: a dyslexia-friendly mode with a different typeface, wider spacing, shorter lines and a cream background.
- **Motion**: switch animations off. If your device is already set to reduce motion, we follow that automatically.

# Where to find it

Learners and parents: open **Settings**. Learners are also asked about this when they first set up their account, and their choices are saved to the account so they follow them to any device they sign in on.

# Also built in

- Every video has captions switched on and a transcript you can read instead.
- Everything works with a keyboard, and there is a "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
- You can zoom the page in your browser as far as you like.

If something is hard to use, tell us with the Report control and choose "Something is wrong or broken".
