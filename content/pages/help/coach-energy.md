Each learner gets an allowance of Coach "energy" every month. The meter in the Coach panel shows how much is left.

# What uses energy

- Sending a message to Coach uses some energy.
- The step's own hints use **none**. Neither do guides, videos, quizzes or anything else in the course.

# When it refills

Energy refills once a month. On a paid plan the month runs from the date the plan started; on Free it is the calendar month. Unused energy does not carry over.

# If you run out

Coach tells you when the refill is due. Everything else carries on working: hints, guides and the course itself. The size of the allowance depends on the family's plan.

# "Coach is taking a break"

There is also a daily limit across the whole site. If it is reached, Coach pauses for everyone and the rest of each course carries on. This is rare, and it is not about you.

# In an emergency

You can still type to Coach when you are out of energy if you need help: a message that suggests someone is in danger always gets the help message. If you are in danger now, call 999.
