Both are on the **Privacy** page of your parent dashboard.

# Download

Ask for a download and we prepare a ZIP file. It contains your child's account details, progress, accepted projects and the files we hold for them, with a README that explains each part.

Your child's private writing (what they typed to Coach, their notes and reflections) is not included in a parent's download; you get the counts, dates and topics. The README says what was left out and how to ask about it.

# Delete

You can ask us to delete one child's account. The parent who set the family up can also delete the whole family.

- The account is closed straight away.
- You have **14 days to undo it**. After that, personal data is deleted or anonymised.
- If you have a paid plan, the subscription is cancelled as part of deleting the family.

A few records are kept after deletion because we have to keep them: payment records, and anything held under our safeguarding policy.

# At 18

An 18-year-old who runs their own account can download or delete their own data from **Settings**, and their download includes everything they wrote.
