Some courses are part of your family's plan and some are not. If a course shows a padlock and "Ask a parent to unlock", it is not on your plan yet.

# What to do

1. Open the course and press **Ask a parent to unlock**.
2. Your parent or carer gets the request in their dashboard and by email.
3. If they say yes and sort it out, the course opens for you. You will see a notice.

# If they say no

You will see their answer. You cannot send the same request again for a week, so pick the ones you really want.

# Meanwhile

Courses marked **Free** are always open to you, and they are full courses with a certificate at the end.

Plans are something your parent or carer looks after, so you will not see anything about them here.
