# Under 13

We cannot open an account for anyone under 13. If the birth month and year show a child is under 13, sign-up stops and nothing about them is saved.

# Why "the 1st of next month"

We ask only for the month and year of birth, never the day. So that nobody is ever treated as older than they are, a birthday counts once that month has finished. A new 13-year-old can join from the 1st of the month after their birthday.

# 13 to 15, and 16 to 17

Some settings are stricter for younger teenagers. For example, a parent completes a card check before a 13 to 15-year-old can use Coach, and Coach's safety checks are tighter for that age group. Each course also has its own minimum age, and a course above your age does not appear for you.

# Turning 18

At 18 you are offered the choice to take over your own account. You can keep the link with your parent or carer, or run the account yourself. An 18-year-old can also create an account without a parent.
