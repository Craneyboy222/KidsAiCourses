A family account is created by a parent or carer aged 18 or over.

# Steps

1. **Create your account** with your email address and a password, and confirm your email from the message we send.
2. **Read how safety and outside tools work.** It is short, and it explains what your child will be doing.
3. **Choose a plan.** You can start on Free.
4. **Add a child.** Give a nickname, pick a username and enter their birth month and year. We do not ask for their email address, full name or school. Usernames are checked so they do not include personal details.
5. **Go through the consent centre** for that child.
6. **Get them signed in.** Either set a password for them now, or give them a one-time invite code. See [invite codes](/help/invite-codes).

You can add more children later from your dashboard, up to the number of places on your plan.

# If your child is under 13

We cannot create the account, and nothing about your child is saved. Accounts open on the 1st of the month after a 13th birthday, because we only ask for the month and year of birth.

# If your child started the sign-up

You will have an email from us with an approval link. See [teen sign-up and parent approval](/help/teen-sign-up).
