The last step of a course asks you to hand in what you made.

# What you can send

It depends on the course. The step tells you which of these it wants:

- **a link** to your project (it must start with https)
- **a screenshot**
- **a file**
- **a video**

# No real people

**Do not send photos or videos that show real people, including you.** Use the sample image the course gives you, a cartoon avatar, an object, or something drawn or generated that does not show a real person. Work that appears to show a real person is not accepted.

# What happens next

1. Your work is checked automatically straight away: file type and size, and whether the link is safe to store.
2. Some work is accepted there and then. Some is checked by a person on our team first. Pictures and videos are always seen by a person, and so is a share of everything else.
3. While you wait, your portfolio shows it as **awaiting review**. You can carry on with other courses.

# If it is sent back

You will get a note saying what to change. The files from that attempt are deleted from our storage, so fix it and send it again.

# Good to know

- We store your link; we never open, host or run your app.
- A certificate is only issued once the work is accepted.
