Coach is the AI helper built into every course. It is an AI, not a person, and it always says so.

# What Coach does

- Starts with the step's own hints, one at a time.
- Asks what you have tried, then gives a nudge, then a clue.
- Explains an idea in a different way, or shows a short example of something similar.
- Quizzes you or gives feedback on what you have made.

# What Coach will not do

- Do the step or the project for you.
- Write homework, coursework, essays or exam answers.
- Sign in to a tool for you, or see your screen in another tab.
- Give medical, legal or money advice.

# Staying safe with Coach

- Do not type personal details such as your full name, address, phone number or school. If we spot some, we remove them before your message is sent, and tell you.
- **Never type a password, sign-in code or key.** Coach will refuse it.
- Coach can be wrong. Check anything important.
- If you tell Coach that you or someone else might be in danger, you will see a message about where to get help straight away, and our safeguarding lead is told so a person can follow up.

# Who can read your chats

Your parent or carer cannot read them. They see how many chats you have had and the topics. What you said is deleted after 30 days unless your parent has chosen to keep chat history for longer.

# If a reply is wrong or upsetting

Press Report on that reply. A person on our team will look at it.
