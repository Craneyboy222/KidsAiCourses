{{brandName}} is an online studio where teenagers aged 13 to 18 learn today's AI tools by making real projects: an app, a short film, a chatbot, a design.

# How a course works

- You pick a project from the [courses](/courses).
- You follow short steps here, and do the making in a real tool in another tab.
- Coach, our built-in AI helper, gives hints if you get stuck. It does not do the work for you.
- At the end you hand in a link, a screenshot or a file. Once it is accepted, it goes in your private portfolio and you get a certificate.

# Who it is for

- **Teenagers aged 13 to 18.** Under-13s cannot have an account.
- **Parents and carers**, who set up or approve the account, choose the limits and see progress.

# What to expect at launch

- Course videos are short, and most steps are written.
- AI helps us draft courses. A person on our team reviews and signs off every course before it is published.
- There are no public profiles, no messaging between learners and no adverts.
