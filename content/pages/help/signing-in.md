# Learners

You sign in with your **username and password**. You do not have an email address on your account, so there is no "email me a reset link".

**Forgotten your password?** Ask your parent or carer. From their dashboard they can set a new one, or give you a one-time [invite code](/help/invite-codes) so you can choose your own.

# Parents and carers

You sign in with your **email address and password**, or ask for a sign-in link to be emailed to you.

**Forgotten your password?** Choose "Forgotten password" on the sign-in page and we will email you a link to set a new one.

# Passwords

- At least 10 characters. Three random words work well.
- We check new passwords against a list of very common ones and refuse those.
- You can paste a password or use a password manager.

# "Sign-in is paused"

After several wrong passwords in a row, sign-in for that account pauses for about 15 minutes. This stops someone guessing. Wait, then try again, or reset the password as above.

# We will never ask for

A password, sign-in code or key for any other website or app. Coach will not ask either. If anything here asks for one, report it.
