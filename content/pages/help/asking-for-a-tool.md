Some tools have rules about age. If a course uses one, you will see it in "Tools you'll need" before you start.

# "Needs a parent's OK"

1. Press the button to send a request.
2. Your parent or carer gets it in their approvals inbox and by email.
3. When they approve, the course opens and you can follow the setup guide.

While you wait, carry on with something else. You will see a notice when they have decided.

# "A parent runs this one"

A few tools are for adults only. Your parent or carer sets up and operates the account, and you direct: you decide what to make, they do the clicking. The guide for that tool is written for them.

# "Not available for your age"

Some tools cannot be used by under-18s at all. A course that depends on one will not be on your list.

# If they say no

That is their call. There are other courses, and many use different tools.
