# Daily time limit

A parent can set a daily limit for each child, anywhere from 5 minutes to 12 hours. Time counts while a learner has {{brandName}} open in a visible tab, on any learner page.

When the limit is reached, the learner's work is saved first and then the session stops for the day. Learners get a warning shortly before.

# Quiet hours

A parent can set hours when learning is switched off, for example overnight. During quiet hours the learner cannot continue a course, and we do not send them notifications. Their progress is saved and waiting.

# Blocking courses or categories

A parent can block a whole category or a single course. Blocked courses do not appear for that child. Progress is kept and comes back if the block is lifted.

# Pausing an account

Pausing signs the child out everywhere straight away. They can still sign in, but they see that the account is paused (with the parent's reason, if one was given) and cannot learn or send anything until it is switched back on. Their work is safe.

# Where to set these

Parents: open your child's page in the dashboard and choose **Controls**.

# For learners

If you have hit your limit or it is quiet time, the screen tells you when you can come back. If you think a limit is wrong, talk to your parent or carer: only they can change it.
