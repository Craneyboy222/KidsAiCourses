You can start your own sign-up, but because you are under 18 a parent or carer has to approve it.

# How it works

1. Enter the month and year you were born, and pick a username. Do not use your real name.
2. Give your parent or carer's email address. We email them a link.
3. Wait for them to approve. Until they do, your account is locked.

# While you are waiting

You can look around and try the public taster of a course. You cannot use Coach, save work or earn a certificate yet.

# If they do not reply

If your account is not approved within **7 days**, we delete it and everything in it. You can sign up again whenever you like.

# Tips

- Tell them the email is coming, so it does not get ignored.
- If it has not arrived, ask them to check their junk folder.

If you are 18, you can create and run your own account without a parent.
