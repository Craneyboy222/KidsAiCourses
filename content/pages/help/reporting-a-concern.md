**If you or someone else is in danger right now, call 999.**

You can also talk to someone free, at any time:

- **Childline**: 0800 1111
- **Samaritans**: 116 123
- **Shout**: text SHOUT to 85258

# The Report control

There is a Report control on every step, every course, every project and every Coach reply. Press it and choose what is wrong:

- **Someone is in danger**: this goes straight to our safeguarding lead.
- **This doesn't match what I see**: the tool's screens look different from our guide.
- **Something is wrong or broken**
- **It's upsetting or not OK**
- **Someone is being unkind**
- **A privacy worry**
- **Something else**

You can add a short note. You do not have to.

# What happens next

A person on our team reads it. If it is about a guide, we check the guide against the tool and fix it. If it is about content, we can take a step or a whole course down while we look. If it is about someone's safety, our safeguarding lead takes over.

We are a small team. We read every report, but not instantly and not through the night, which is why the numbers above come first.

Learners can see the reports they have made under **Settings**.

# For parents

Safety notices about your child appear on your dashboard and are emailed to you straight away. If our safeguarding lead needs to speak to you, they will contact you directly.
