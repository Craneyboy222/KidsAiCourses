# Steps

A course is a list of steps. Some you watch or read, some are setup guides, some send you to a tool to make something, and some ask you a question. At launch the videos are short and most steps are written.

# Saving

Everything saves by itself: your place, your ticks and your notes. Close the tab, come back on another device, and you carry on from the same step.

# Skipping

You can skip ahead on most steps. Skipped steps are marked so you can find them again. You cannot skip the final hand-in, and a course is only finished when every step is done.

# Videos

Captions are on by default and every video has a transcript. You can change the speed, and videos never start playing sound on their own.

# Quizzes

No timers, and you can retry as many times as you like. You get feedback as soon as you check your answers. They are there to help you learn, not to catch you out.

# The ethics checkpoint

Every course has one. It is a short stop to think about things like whose work an AI was trained on, what is fair to copy, and being honest about what AI did and what you did. What you write there is private to you.

# Before you hand in

You check your own work against a short list. Choosing "Not yet" does not stop you handing in. It is there to help you spot what to improve.

# Stuck?

Press "I'm stuck". You get the step's hints first, then [Coach](/help/how-coach-works).
