Plans and current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing). This article explains how billing works.

# Who pays

Only a parent, a carer or an adult learner ever sees prices or a checkout. Learners under 18 see "Ask a parent to unlock" instead.

# Free and paid

- **Free** is not a trial and does not run out. It includes the courses marked Free, a Coach allowance, the portfolio, certificates for those courses and every parental control.
- **A paid family plan** opens every course and gives each learner a bigger Coach allowance.

# Starting, trials and promo codes

Choose a plan from **Billing** in your dashboard. You finish on a secure page run by Stripe, the company that handles card payments for us; we never see or store your card number. If a free trial is offered, a card is needed to start it and a family can have one trial. A promo code is entered at this step, by you.

# Cancelling or changing

Open **Billing** and choose to manage your plan. You can cancel or update your card at any time.

# If a payment fails

We tell you and nothing changes for a short grace period while the payment is retried. If it still has not gone through after that, the family moves to Free until a payment succeeds.

# When a plan ends

Nothing is deleted. Finished projects, portfolios and certificates are all kept, and the free courses stay open.

# The card check for 13 to 15-year-olds

Separately from any plan, we ask the parent of a 13 to 15-year-old to complete a card check before the child uses Coach. No money is taken. Starting a paid plan does the same job.

# Costs outside {{brandName}}

Tools from other companies are separate. Courses are designed to be finished on a tool's free plan, and where something costs money the course says so before your child starts.
