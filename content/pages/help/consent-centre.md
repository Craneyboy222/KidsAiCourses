Before your child's account is used, we ask for your consent in plain English. You can read and change your choices at any time from your child's page in the dashboard.

# Needed for an account to work

- **Account and learning data.** The nickname and username, birth month and year, courses, progress, quiz answers, notes and the work they hand in.
- **Coach, our built-in AI helper.** Messages your child types to Coach are checked, have personal details we can detect removed, and are sent to an AI company that works for us under contract.
- **Using tools from other companies.** Courses teach real outside tools, each with its own terms. Agreeing to this does not approve any particular tool: you are asked about each one that needs it.

# Optional, and off unless you switch them on

- **Keep Coach conversations for longer** (90 days instead of 30).
- **Help us improve the courses.** Records of how your child moves through courses, kept under a random code rather than their name.

# Changing your mind

You can switch an optional consent off at any time and nothing else changes.

If you withdraw a consent the account needs, the account is **paused straight away** and your child is signed out. We ask you to confirm first. Their work is kept safe until you give consent again or ask us to delete the account.

# If the wording changes

Consent wording is versioned. If we change the wording of a consent the account needs, we ask you to read and agree to the new version.
