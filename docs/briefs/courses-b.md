## Every course
3–5 modules; a first win within about 10 minutes; consistent minutes; `sessions`, careers, skills, interests (from the 16 tags), pathways; a project brief with a concrete scenario, deliverables, constraints and stretch goals; a rubric of 3–4 criteria with levels "Not yet", "Getting there", "Nailed it"; at least one quiz with feedback on every option, using `spot_the_problem`, `order` and `multi` where they fit; a meaningful `ethics_checkpoint` tied to the project; a `checkpoint` in every module; a `reflection`; exactly one `submit` as the final step with `requireRubricSelfCheck: true` and a clear `evidenceHint`. A full setup guide for every required tool (`create_account`, `verify_email`, `choose_free_plan`, `privacy_settings`) and real `external_task` steps with `task` and `clicks`. Completable on the free plan, or the parent cost stated and flagged. Tiered hints that never give the full solution, and a short `coachContext` on working steps. Trailer and any video step reference placeholder assets in `content/assets/<short-name>.json` (video, captions, transcript, poster; images with alt text; `placeholder: true`; no binaries). `watchOut` where the spec flags one.

## Safety, non-negotiable
Evidence never requires images or video of real people: No real-person likeness, no voice cloning, original or rights-cleared audio, a "Made with AI" end card, no copied logos, no personal contact details, private or unlisted sharing, never paste keys or passwords into a tool, no real payments. No step may encourage passing AI work off as the learner's own at school. British English in the house voice.

## Course-specific
- **Spot the Deepfake** has no external tool: `read`, `video`, `quiz`, `checkpoint`, `ethics_checkpoint`, `reflection`, optional `upload`, `submit`. Its curated media set is placeholder platform assets described so a producer can make or license them: synthetic or illustrated FICTIONAL people and scenes, each labelled as made for the course — never fakes of real, identifiable people. Submission is the learner's spotting checklist. Say in your report what the owner must commission or license.
- **The Citation Machine**: every submitted citation is one the learner opened and checked; the course teaches spotting invented references; honesty beats volume in the rubric.
- **Side-Hustle**: "customers" are people the learner already knows offline, with a parent aware; no strangers, public posts, real payments or personal data. Everything commercial stays on paper.
- **Portfolio and CV Studio**: no address, phone, school or date of birth on anything public; private or unlisted site; CV as PDF; no photo of the learner — initials, a monogram or an illustrated avatar.
- **Stats Detective**: an open or provided dataset as a `dataset_csv` placeholder asset with its columns and a note on where a suitably licensed real dataset could come from; separate what the numbers show from opinion; no betting content.
