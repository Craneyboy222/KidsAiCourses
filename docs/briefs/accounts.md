## Requirements
- Sign-up refuses while `isSignupOpen` is false: friendly "not open yet" page and the `signup.closed_attempt` counter.
- **Under-13 block**: if birth month and year give an age under 13, stop BEFORE anything is written — no account, username, parent email or log line about the child. Only the anonymous `signup.under13_blocked` counter moves; show `/blocked-age` (friendly, blames nobody, collects nothing, no "notify me"); set a short-lived cookie holding only a flag to stop an immediate retry. Applies to parent-added children and teen-first sign-up.
- Parent-led: an adult self-declares 18+, signs up with email and password or a magic link, verifies the email, a family is created; adds child profiles (nickname, birth month and year, username; never a child email) within the plan's seats; sets a password or issues a one-time invite code (hashed, single use, 72 hours, attempts counted and rate limited). `resetChildPassword(ctx, learnerId)` for the parent dashboard revokes the child's sessions.
- Usernames: 3–20 letters, digits, underscores; case-insensitive unique; refuse personal information (nickname, email, phone, birth year, school- or place-like words) and profanity (`obscenity`); offer three generated safe suggestions.
- Teen-first: birth month and year, username, parent's email → `pending_parent`, locked (no Coach, no saved work, no certificate); parent gets `parent_approval_request` with a hashed token valid 7 days; the teen is told plainly the account is deleted after 7 days; job `accounts.purge_pending` (daily) deletes everything about unapproved accounts. `/approve/[token]`: the parent creates or signs in to a parent account, the teen joins the family (seat limits apply), the parent completes consent, then the teen is `active`.
- Adult (18) self sign-up when `site.adult_self_serve` is on: own verified email, own single-seat family they own.
- Consent centre: required `account_learning_data`, `ai_processing`, `third_party_tools`; optional `tutor_history_retention`, `product_improvement_analytics` (both default off for 13–15). Definitions are versioned data in `content/consents` with plain-English copy for parents. Every grant and revocation appends a `consent_record` (who, when, method, version). A new version of a required definition needs re-consent. Revoking a required consent pauses the learner with a clear explanation and ends their sessions; re-granting un-pauses only if nothing else holds the account.
- Age assurance per `safety.age_assurance_mode`: `card_or_subscription` → a 13–15-year-old's parent needs a £0 card authorisation or an active paid subscription before Coach; 16–17 need the parent's verified email and consent; 18 needs nothing more. `recordAgeAssurance` is called by payments. Leave `TODO(legal)`; do not hard-code a legal conclusion.
- Onboarding. Learner: generated avatar (seed only), at least three of the 16 interest tags, accessibility preferences, first-project pick; skippable after interests. Parent: reads how safety and external tools work, chooses Free or the Family trial (link to the billing page owned elsewhere), adds a child, completes consent.
- `accounts.recalculate_bands` (daily): recompute `safety_band` and default `pathway`; birthday-transition notices; at 18 offer independence. Independence: the 18-year-old adds and verifies their own email and becomes owner of their own single-seat family; the parent link becomes optional; nothing changes until they confirm.
- Sign-in: parents by email and password or magic link; learners by username and password; staff by email and password then TOTP (handle `twoFactorRedirect`; first-time enrolment with backup codes). Calm lockout message. On success copy saved accessibility preferences into the `a11y` cookie and route by kind using `safeNextPath`: learner → `/onboarding` or `/home`, parent → `/parent/onboarding` or `/parent`, staff → `/studio`.
- Password reset: parents and adult learners by email at `/reset`; a teen's is reset by their parent (the teen page explains that).
- Forms use the kit (`Field`, `MonthYearPicker`, `PasswordInput`, `SubmitButton`, `useActionForm`), work without JavaScript where practical, and never ask a child for an email address, full date of birth, real name, photo, school or location. Under-18s never see prices, plans or checkout. No service ever returns or logs the learner placeholder email.
- Copy keys for "how safety and external tools work" and "What my parent can see" in `src/content/copy/en-GB.ts` for other slices to reuse.
