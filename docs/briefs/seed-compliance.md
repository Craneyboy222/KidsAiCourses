Spec to read in full: Section 15 (seed), Section 9 (retention defaults, compliance), Section 10 and 11 (stack, repo layout, docs), Section 14 (environment), Section 16 (CI), Section 17 and 18, Section 20 (definition of done).

## Owns
`scripts/seed.ts` (`pnpm db:seed`), `scripts/make-placeholder-assets.ts`, `src/server/jobs/retention.ts` and `src/server/services/retention.ts`, `content/help` is NOT yours (marketing owns it). Docs: `README.md`, `docs/RUNBOOK.md`, `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`, `docs/compliance/**` (DPIA template, record of processing, retention schedule, children's code checklist, Online Safety Act notes, processor register, incident and breach procedure, safeguarding procedure outline), `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` tidy-up, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `vercel.json`. Tests in `tests/unit/seed`, `tests/integration/seed`.

## Requirements
- `pnpm db:migrate && pnpm db:seed` on an empty database gives an explorable local environment and is idempotent. It refuses to run when `APP_ENV` is production or preview. It creates: the owner (`OWNER_BOOTSTRAP_EMAIL`, `super_admin`) and one staff account per role; two families (one Free, one Family plan via the fake Stripe driver) with learners aged 13, 15, 16 and 17, an 18-year-old with their own account, and one pending teen; plans and promo samples; consent definitions and granted consents; the Tool Register, assets and all courses in `content/` published through the real approval path (`importContent` with `publishAsSeed`); the 175 ideas; pages (marketing, help, legal drafts — the dev seed may publish the legal drafts with the draft banner so local sign-up can open); prompt and email templates; feature flags; retention policies; some realistic progress, one accepted and one pending submission, a certificate, moderation examples and a test safeguarding case; one sample `ai_draft` course waiting in the review queue; and `markLaunchReadyForDev` plus sign-up open. Every password is `SEED_PASSWORD`. Print a short table of accounts at the end. Use the services, not raw inserts, wherever a service exists; where a slice is not built yet, skip that part with a clear message rather than failing.
- Placeholder assets: generate with ffmpeg (already installed) a short title-card MP4, a WebVTT captions file, a Markdown transcript, a poster PNG and any sample images for every asset marked `placeholder: true` in `content/assets/*.json`, write them to storage under their keys, and never overwrite a real asset. Sample images must be illustrations made by the script (shapes, an abstract avatar), never a photo of a person.
- Retention: `retention_policy` rows with the spec's defaults (Coach messages 30 or 90 days; flagged conversations under the safeguarding schedule; pending teen accounts 7 days; deleted accounts after the undo window; inactive accounts with warnings before deletion; logs and audit periods; rejected uploads immediately). A daily job applies each policy, in batches, deleting stored files as well as rows, never touching append-only records, and writing counts to `job_run`. A dry-run mode for Studio. Periods the law decides are marked `⚖️`.
- Compliance documents: honest templates filled in from what the product really does (data categories, purposes, lawful-basis placeholders, processors and regions, retention, children's code standards one by one, risk tables), with every legal judgement marked `⚖️ TO CONFIRM`. Do not invent legal conclusions.
- `README.md`: what it is, quick start, commands, test databases, where things live. `docs/RUNBOOK.md`: incidents, the kill switches, taking a course down, a safeguarding alert, restoring from backup, rotating keys. `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`: Vercel (London region), database, storage, email, Inngest and Stripe set-up step by step, the environment variables, the go-live checklist — written for the owner to approve; nothing is deployed by you.
- CI workflow: install, typecheck, lint, content check, unit and integration tests against a Postgres service, build, Playwright and axe.

## Tests required
Seed runs twice without error or duplicates; seed refuses in production; retention deletes what is due and nothing else, including files; append-only tables untouched; placeholder generator skips real assets.
