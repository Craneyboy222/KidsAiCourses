
## Course content model (raised by the content-model task, 15 November 2026)

- **Snapchat tagline is over the limit.** The example in spec 6.1 has a 134-character tagline; the model says 120 or fewer. The limit was kept and the tagline in `content/courses/build-your-own-snapchat-style-app.json` shortened to "Build a camera app with your own filters and disappearing messages in a real coding tool. AI helps — you stay in charge." (120). Confirm the wording, or raise the limit.
- **Snapchat step s5 says "download it from this step".** The sample image now sits on the new read step before it (s16), as briefed, and s5 was left exactly as the spec has it. Either reword s5 to "from the previous step" or also attach `img-snaplab-sample` to s5.
- **`order` quiz questions.** The spec's option shape has no position field, so the authored order of the options is taken as the answer and every option is marked `correct`. `toLearnerCourse` scrambles the options so the order is not leaked. Confirm, or add an explicit position field.
- **Real-people rule also catches invitations.** "Add a photo of yourself if you like" fails validation as well as "upload a selfie", because the upload would still arrive (safer reading of spec 3.4 and D3). The check is a text heuristic and a backstop only; the reviewer checklist item and moderation still apply.
- **`pnpm content:check` without `--strict`** reports a tool with no under-18 policy as a warning ("to verify before publish"), as briefed. It treats a tool that has a policy but is not yet approved the same way. `--strict` makes both errors. CI should run `--strict` before anything is published.

## Database and authentication foundation (raised by the database and auth task, 15 November 2026)

- **Learner email placeholder.** Better Auth 1.7.25 does not allow `user.email` to be null: the core schema marks it `required: true, unique: true`, validates it as a plain string, and several flows (sign-in, the TOTP label, email verification) read it without a null check. Learners therefore hold an internal, non-routable placeholder, `<random uuid>@learner.invalid` (`.invalid` is reserved by RFC 2606 and can never receive mail; the uuid is random, not the user id). It is hidden behind `userEmail()` in `src/server/auth/learner-email.ts`, stripped from every Better Auth response, refused on every email route (sign-in, reset, verification, magic link), never sent to, and a database check stops it sitting on a parent or staff account. The spec's "email nullable" (Section 12) is met in effect rather than in the column. Revisit if a later Better Auth release supports a null email.
- **Age inside the birth month: the younger reading was taken. Please confirm.** We hold only birth month and year, so for the whole of the birth month we cannot know whether the birthday has happened. `ageFromBirth` counts the birthday only once the birth month is completely over in Europe/London (from the 1st of the following month). So a child born in November 2013 is treated as 12, and blocked at sign-up, until 1 December 2026, even if their birthday was on 2 November. A learner can be treated as up to a month younger than they are and never older. This is the safe direction for the under-13 block, the 13–15 band, `minAge` and what an 18-year-old may do alone, and it follows "when in doubt choose the younger age". The cost is that a just-turned-13 may have to wait up to a month, so the block screen copy should say "you can join from the 1st of next month". If the owner prefers the birthday to count from the first day of the birth month, change one comparison in `src/server/services/age.ts` (`month > birthMonth` to `month >= birthMonth`) and the matching tests; that reading would let some 12-year-olds in for up to a month. ⚖️
- **Safety band on the Actor is the stricter of the stored and the live value.** The stored `learner_profile.safety_band` is refreshed by the daily job; the Actor's band is calculated live from the birth month and year and, if the two disagree, the younger band wins until the job catches up (so an 18th birthday does not unlock anything before the transition job has run and sent its notice).
- **Brief sign-in lock uses `user.locked_until`, not `status = 'locked'`.** Five wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock sign-in for 15 minutes. It does not end a session that is already signed in (otherwise anyone who knows a username could kick a learner out mid-lesson) and it never overwrites a deliberate pause or lock. `status = 'locked'` remains the deliberate lock set by a parent or staff, which does sign the user out. While an account is locked the sign-in form answers "locked" for that username whatever the password; this does confirm that the username exists, which was judged better than letting the lock leak whether a guessed password was right.
- **Notifying the parent of a lock is not wired.** `notifyParentOfLockedAccount` in `src/server/auth/lockout.ts` is called at the moment of the lock and does nothing until the notifications system registers a handler with `setAccountLockedHandler(...)`.
- **`STAFF_2FA_REQUIRED=false` has little effect today.** `requireStaff()` honours the flag, and the Actor's `twoFactorVerified` always tells the truth, but `can()` in `src/server/authz` refuses staff without a second factor regardless of the flag. That is the safe default. If local development or end-to-end tests need staff access without TOTP, the lead should decide where to relax it (the flag is not checked in production configuration either: consider refusing `STAFF_2FA_REQUIRED=false` when `APP_ENV=production` in `src/env.ts`).
- **Staff cannot use magic links or "trust this device".** Both would create a session that skips the second factor, so magic links are sent to parent accounts only and the trust-device option is ignored.
- **Session lengths for learners and parents** (the spec gives a figure only for staff): seven days, renewed on use, with a hard limit of 30 days before signing in again. Staff: 12 hours from sign-in, enforced in the session hook, by a database trigger and again in `getCtx`. Confirm the learner figure suits shared school Chromebooks, or shorten it.
- **Append-only tables and retention.** `consent_record`, `credit_ledger` and `audit_log` refuse UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE. Retention still requires deleting old rows (audit logs after two years, a deleted learner's ledger), so there is one sanctioned route: `withAppendOnlyMaintenance()` switches the block off for DELETE and TRUNCATE inside a single transaction. UPDATE is never possible. These tables carry ids without foreign keys, so a consent record outlives the account as pseudonymous evidence. How long evidence of consent is kept after an account is deleted is ⚖️.
- **Safeguarding cases outlive the account** (`learner_id` is set to null on deletion; the encrypted notes remain until the DSL retention policy removes them). ⚖️ Confirm with the DSL policy.
- **`user.kind` is text, not an enum,** because Section 12 asks for it to stay open. An unknown kind is treated as signed out everywhere.
- **Sub-penny AI costs.** Whole pence cannot represent the cost of one model call, so `ai_request.cost_millipence` and `spend_counter.cost_millipence` count thousandths of a penny. Everything else is integer pence.
- **Better Auth owns a sixth table.** `auth_rate_limit` backs Better Auth's own rate limiter for `/api/auth/*` (switched on in production and preview only, so local runs and end-to-end tests are not throttled). Our own limits use `rate_limit_bucket`.

## Design system, UI kit and layout shells (raised by the UI kit task, 15 November 2026)

- **Every page is now rendered per request.** The brief asked for the accessibility preferences to be read from the `a11y` cookie in the root layout, so that the theme is right in the first byte of HTML with no flash and no inline script. Reading a cookie in the root layout makes every route dynamic, including marketing pages, so `revalidate` on a marketing page will have no effect (ARCHITECTURE.md says marketing pages "may use `revalidate`"). If cached marketing pages matter more than a flash-free first paint for signed-out visitors, the alternative is a small inline script in `<head>` that copies the cookie onto `<html>`; that needs a Content-Security-Policy decision. Lead to choose.
- **The `a11y` cookie and signed-in users.** The root layout reads only the cookie. For a signed-in learner whose preferences live in `learner_profile.a11y`, sign-in (and a profile change on another device) should write the same JSON to the `a11y` cookie, so the two stay in step. `A11yPreferences` already calls `onSave` for the profile half. The cookie is first-party, holds four settings and no identifier, and is only written when someone changes a setting; it should be listed in the cookies page as a preference cookie the user sets themselves. ⚖️ Confirm it sits within "strictly necessary" for learners.
- **Urgent-help wording in `ReportButton`.** When a learner picks a reason marked `urgent` ("Someone is in danger") the dialog shows a default line: call 999 if someone is in danger now, and Childline on 0800 1111. It is a prop (`urgentHelp`) so the DSL-approved crisis wording can replace it. ⚖️ The default should be reviewed with the crisis scripts before launch.
- **Legal and help link targets in `MarketingShell`** are guesses from the spec's list (`/legal/privacy`, `/legal/privacy-teens`, `/legal/terms`, `/legal/cookies`, `/legal/community-code`, `/legal/ai-use`). They are exported constants and props; whoever builds the legal pages should correct the slugs.
- **`AskParentToUnlock` drops copy that mentions money.** A `title` or `description` containing a currency sign, "GBP", pence, or "per month / a year" wording is replaced by the default text, so a price cannot reach a learner through this component by mistake. It is a text check and a backstop only: the page must still not pass prices to learner screens.
- **Open tooltips and dropdown menus are flagged by axe's `region` rule** (best practice, moderate): Radix renders them at the end of `<body>`, outside the page landmarks. Dialogs, sheets and popovers are not affected. No serious or critical violations are reported. Left as is, because rendering them inline risks clipping inside scrolling containers.
- **Scaffold leftovers.** `public/next.svg`, `vercel.svg`, `file.svg`, `globe.svg` and `window.svg` are no longer referenced by anything and can be deleted. They were left in place because they are outside the UI kit's files.
- **Check the client bundle once `next build` is allowed.** The kit has one barrel, `@/components/ui`. Server Components can import from it freely. A Client Component that imports from the barrel relies on the bundler dropping what it does not use; if a production build shows `react-markdown` (the largest part of the kit) in a client chunk that does not render Markdown, import that page's components from their own files (`@/components/ui/button`) or move `Markdown` out of the barrel. This could not be measured here because the task was told not to run `next build`.
